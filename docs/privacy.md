# Privacy policy

Open Headphones Connect does not collect, send or share any personal information. That goes for
the Windows app and the Mac app alike.

## What the app uses

- **Your Bluetooth headphones.** The app talks to Sony headphones you have paired with your
  computer, to read their battery, noise control and settings and to change them when you ask.
  This happens directly between your computer and your headphones. On a Mac, macOS asks you
  first whether the app may use Bluetooth.
- **Files on your computer.** On Windows the app keeps its settings, and a log of what the
  headphones reported to help with problems, in your Windows user folder. You can delete them
  at any time; uninstalling the app removes them. On a Mac the app keeps only its settings, in
  your Library folder, and no log.

## What the app does not do

- It does not connect to the internet, and has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
- It does not read your contacts, files, location, microphone or camera.

The Windows app's log stays on your PC. It only leaves your PC if you choose to attach it to a
problem report yourself; it contains your headphones' model, firmware version and settings, and
nothing about you.
