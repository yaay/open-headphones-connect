# Privacy policy

Open Headphones Connect does not collect, send or share any personal information.

## What the app uses

- **Your Bluetooth headphones.** The app talks to Sony headphones you have paired with Windows,
  to read their battery, noise control and settings and to change them when you ask. This
  happens directly between your PC and your headphones.
- **Files on your PC.** The app keeps its settings, and a log of what the headphones reported to
  help with problems, in your Windows user folder. You can delete them at any time; uninstalling
  the app removes them.

## What the app does not do

- It does not connect to the internet, and has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
- It does not read your contacts, files, location, microphone or camera.

The log stays on your PC. It only leaves your PC if you choose to attach it to a problem report
yourself; it contains your headphones' model, firmware version and settings, and nothing about
you.
