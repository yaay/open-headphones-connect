## Product name

Open Headphones Connect

## Description

Open Headphones Connect brings your Sony wireless headphones to the Windows taskbar.

When your headphones connect, a small card slides in with a 3D picture of them, the battery level and the noise mode, then gets out of your way. After that, everything lives in an icon next to the clock: click it to see the battery, switch between noise cancelling and ambient sound, adjust the equalizer, or change settings you would otherwise need your phone for.

What you can do:
• See the battery level at a glance, right in the notification area
• Switch between noise cancelling, ambient sound and off
• Pick an equalizer preset or shape your own curve
• Play, pause, skip tracks and change the volume
• Connect, disconnect or power off the headphones with one click
• Change headphone settings: Speak-to-Chat, touch panel, DSEE upscaling, voice guidance, automatic power-off
• Get a warning when the battery runs low
• Start quietly with Windows

Headphones it works with:
• WH-1000XM6, WH-1000XM4, WH-1000XM3: tested and working
• ULT WEAR: mostly working
• WH-1000XM5, WF-1000XM4 and XM5, LinkBuds and other recent Sony models: expected to work, not yet tested
The app shows only the controls your headphones actually have. Headphones from other brands are not supported.

Private by design: the app talks only to your headphones. It does not connect to the internet, and has no account, ads or tracking.

Free and open source. The code, the full list of supported headphones and the place to report problems are at https://github.com/yaay/open-headphones-connect

Open Headphones Connect is an unofficial, community-made app. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Sony. Your headphones must already be paired with Windows over Bluetooth.

3D model on the connect card: "Sony Wh-1000xm5" by Zalaneke, licensed under CC BY 4.0.
